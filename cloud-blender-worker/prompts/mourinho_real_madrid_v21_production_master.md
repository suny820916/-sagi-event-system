# MOURINHO REAL MADRID — PRODUCTION MASTER PROMPT V2.1

## Publication title
穆里尼奥的皇马，真正危险的是什么？

## Publication subtitle
高位压迫 · 3秒转换 · 紧凑防守

Create a 70-second tactical animation explaining José Mourinho’s 2026/27 Real Madrid.

The animation should follow a clean, premium tactical visualization style inspired by Teactics.

Do not add any extra text, captions, logos, or labels inside the animation.

The animation must explain one complete tactical cause-and-effect sequence.

This is NOT a football highlight animation.

Do not dramatize the final shot.

The tactical process is more important than the finish.

## CORE IDEA

“Mourinho’s Real Madrid are not defending all the time.

They are constantly preparing for the few seconds in which the opponent becomes most vulnerable.”

## BASE FORMATION

Use a 4-2-3-1 structure.

Real Madrid:

GK: Courtois

RB: Valverde
RCB: Konaté
LCB: Huijsen
LB: Cucurella

DM: Tchouaméni
DM: Bernardo Silva

RW: Arda Güler
AM: Bellingham
LW: Diomande

ST: Mbappé

## 0–7 SEC — ESTABLISH THE STRUCTURE

Begin with a full-pitch tactical view.

Use a relatively high camera angle.

Do not zoom in too early.

Allow the viewer to clearly read the full Real Madrid 4-2-3-1 structure.

The back four maintain horizontal spacing.

Tchouaméni and Bernardo Silva stay in front of the defensive line.

Bellingham operates between the lines.

Güler and Diomande provide width.

Mbappé stays as the highest player.

Do not highlight any player initially.

Hold the complete structure briefly.

The opponent begins building from the back.

## 7–20 SEC — CLOSE THE CENTRE FIRST

The opponent’s centre-back receives the ball.

Mbappé must NOT immediately sprint toward the ball.

His first responsibility is to block the passing lane into central midfield.

Bellingham protects the next central passing option.

Güler and Diomande position themselves between the opposition full-backs and midfielders.

The entire purpose is to deny central progression.

Slightly lower the camera.

Move from a high top-down view into a diagonal tactical angle.

Show enough surrounding players to make the structure readable.

Briefly show a subtle central restriction zone.

Avoid excessive arrows.

The opponent should gradually recognize that the centre is unavailable.

The opponent is forced toward the flank.

## 20–31 SEC — PRESSING TRIGGER

The ball is played toward the opposition left-back.

This pass activates the press.

The animation tempo increases immediately.

Güler attacks the ball carrier.

Mbappé curves his run from inside to outside and removes the return pass toward the centre-back.

Bellingham steps forward and blocks the opposition defensive midfielder.

Valverde aggressively pushes forward from right-back.

These four movements must happen almost simultaneously.

The important tactical idea is:

the opponent is not being chased.

the opponent is being trapped.

Highlight only these four Real Madrid players.

Reduce visual emphasis on everyone else.

Use a medium tactical camera.

Keep the touchline, halfway line, ball carrier, nearby midfielders and centre-back inside the frame.

Do not over-zoom.

The viewer must clearly see all safe exits disappearing.

## 31–36 SEC — FORCE THE ERROR

The opponent hesitates.

The passing options have disappeared.

Under pressure, the full-back attempts a rushed diagonal pass back inside.

Do not cut away.

Show the entire pass.

Tchouaméni has already anticipated the passing lane.

He steps forward before the ball arrives.

He intercepts cleanly.

Use a very short tactical hold after the interception.

Maximum hold:

0.5–0.7 seconds.

Do not freeze the whole sequence.

This is only a visual punctuation mark.

The animation must immediately move into transition.

## 36–45 SEC — TRANSITION BEGINS

Tchouaméni’s first intention is forward.

No sideways circulation.

No unnecessary control touches.

He immediately plays into Bellingham.

Before receiving the ball, Bellingham has already opened his body.

At the same time:

Güler moves wider to the right.

Diomande accelerates forward on the left.

Both movements stretch the recovering defensive line.

Mbappé initially remains close to the centre-back.

He must NOT sprint too early.

The centre-back should briefly believe Mbappé wants the ball to feet.

This creates the tactical deception.

## 45–50 SEC — CREATE THE DEPTH

Bellingham receives the ball.

Mbappé immediately spins behind the centre-back.

The acceleration must be sudden.

Show a subtle vertical attacking corridor.

The order is critical:

Mbappé changes direction.

The defensive line reacts.

The space opens.

Bellingham recognizes the movement.

Then Bellingham plays the through ball.

The pass must be caused by the run.

Never animate the pass first and invent the run afterward.

The camera starts moving vertically with the attack.

Do not move sideways.

Keep Mbappé, the recovering centre-back and the ball visible simultaneously.

## 50–53 SEC — MBAPPÉ FINISHES IMMEDIATELY

THIS ACTION MUST BE VERY FAST.

Do not turn the final shot into a separate cinematic sequence.

Mbappé receives the through ball while already moving at speed.

He takes a maximum of one control touch.

Then he shoots.

Maximum time from Mbappé receiving the ball to the shot:

2.0–2.5 seconds.

Maximum number of touches:

1 control touch + 1 shot.

Preferably:

first touch into space,

second action is the finish.

NO hesitation.

NO slowing down.

NO extra dribbling.

NO waiting for the goalkeeper.

NO long shooting preparation.

NO exaggerated backswing.

NO slow-motion effect.

NO dramatic camera orbit.

NO close-up of Mbappé before shooting.

NO long pause between his final touch and the shot.

The goalkeeper may step forward naturally.

The centre-back continues recovering.

Mbappé finishes before the defensive structure can reset.

The shot itself should take less than one second.

After the shot:

hold for approximately 0.3–0.5 seconds only.

Immediately leave the scene.

Do not show celebration.

Do not replay the finish.

The tactical point is the SPEED of the transition, not the beauty of the shot.

## IMPORTANT TRANSITION TIMING

From Tchouaméni’s interception to Mbappé’s shot:

approximately 5–7 seconds of animation time.

The internal football action should communicate an even faster real-game transition.

The entire attack should feel like one continuous acceleration.

There must be no visible pause between:

interception,

forward pass,

Bellingham receiving,

Mbappé running,

through ball,

shot.

## 53–70 SEC — COMPACT DEFENSIVE BLOCK

Move immediately into another situation.

This time Real Madrid attempt to press high but the opponent escapes.

Do not allow Real Madrid to continue chasing.

The team immediately recognizes that the high press has failed.

Everyone drops.

Diomande recovers on the left.

Güler recovers on the right.

Bellingham drops toward midfield.

The team reorganizes into a compact 4-4-1-1.

Mbappé remains highest.

Bellingham positions underneath him.

The defensive line and midfield line quickly reduce the vertical distance between them.

Return to a higher tactical camera.

The viewer must clearly see the transformation from:

high pressing structure

into

compact defensive structure.

The opponent moves the ball horizontally.

Real Madrid shift together.

Do not animate individual chasing.

The entire block moves as one unit.

When the ball reaches the flank:

the full-back steps out,

the nearest midfielder protects the inside channel,

the centre-back protects the space behind,

the weak-side players move inward.

Show the block becoming narrower around the ball.

The centre of the pitch must remain protected.

Hold the final compact structure for approximately one second.

The final image should clearly communicate:

Real Madrid are organized,

the centre is closed,

and the opponent is being forced away from dangerous space.

## CORE TACTICAL LOGIC

This Real Madrid side does not press high continuously.

It does not defend deep continuously either.

The system is based on collective synchronization.

When the team presses, everyone moves together.

When the first press fails, everyone drops together.

The high press begins by protecting the centre.

The opponent is then guided toward a predetermined area.

Only then does the aggressive press begin.

The press is designed to produce a predictable pass.

Tchouaméni anticipates that pass.

Once possession is regained, the objective changes instantly.

There is no unnecessary possession phase.

The team immediately searches for the space behind the defensive line.

Bellingham connects the transition.

Güler and Diomande stretch the defensive structure.

Mbappé attacks depth.

The finish is only the final consequence.

The true tactical action is everything that created the shot.

## CRITICAL ANIMATION RULE

DO NOT allocate animation time equally to every football action.

Tactical importance determines screen time.

The press may require several seconds because the viewer needs to understand the structure.

The final shot does NOT require several seconds.

A simple finish should remain simple.

If Mbappé is already through on goal, complete the action immediately.

Never stretch a football action simply because there is unused timeline duration.

The camera must explain tactics.

The camera must not simply follow the ball.

Formation explanation:

high tactical view.

medium diagonal tactical view.

Transition:

camera moves forward along the vertical pitch axis.

Final finish:

slightly closer if necessary, but still tactical.

Do not switch to cinematic close-up mode.

The final shot must remain part of the tactical sequence.

For every important pass, show:

the passer,

the available space,

the ball movement,

the receiver,

and the receiver’s first action.

Never move the camera to the receiver before the pass actually travels.

Do not anticipate future action with the camera.
