# SAGI KIT Brand Mark

## Official primary mark
The approved SAGI KIT symbol is the **A-shaped white arch + red dot**.

### Rules
- The A-shaped arch is the primary silhouette.
- The red dot is a fixed brand memory point and must not be removed in normal full-color use.
- On dark interfaces, use the white arch + red dot.
- At small sizes, use the symbol alone; do not force the full wordmark into tight spaces.
- "SAGI KIT" is a product/business name next to the mark, not a replacement for the mark.
- Customer-facing KIT pages must not display factory/supplier marks as SAGI branding.
- Supplier names, source model IDs, costs, and factory contacts stay outside the public customer bundle.

### Core placements
1. Mobile launcher/header
2. Customer selection flow
3. Customer confirmation sheet
4. Order history/workflow center
5. Admin import bridge
6. Cloud backup settings
7. Future jersey chest mark / shorts mark / neck mark / packaging

### Color
- Arch: #FFFFFF on dark surfaces
- Dot: #FF1717
- Dark background recommendation: #080A0D


## Garment placement standard V1.0

### 01 Right chest
- Adult: 45–50 mm wide
- Youth: 38–43 mm wide
- Visual alignment: level with the team crest on the left chest
- Keep at least 25 mm from neck/sleeve seam

### 02 Back neck
- Adult: 22–28 mm wide
- Youth: 20–24 mm wide
- 18–25 mm below collar edge
- Symbol only: A + red dot

### 03 Shorts
- Adult: 35–42 mm wide
- Youth: 30–36 mm wide
- Same side as the jersey right-chest mark
- Keep at least 30 mm above hem

### 04 Hangtag
- Recommended finished size: 55 × 90 mm
- Front: A + red dot
- Back: SAGI KIT / size / SAGI style code / care information
- Never show supplier identity, source style code, cost or supplier contact

### 05 Packaging
- Main mark: 70–100 mm wide
- Use center or fixed upper-left brand zone
- Shipping label may include team / customer / size / quantity / SAGI order ID
- Supplier information must remain private

### Production rule
For dark garments use white A + red dot. For light garments a black A may be used, while the red dot remains red. Team crest stays on left chest; SAGI primary garment mark stays on right chest.
